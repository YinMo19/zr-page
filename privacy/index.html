Zero Reader Effective date: June 9, 2026

Privacy Policy

Zero Reader is designed as a local reading app. It does not collect, track, sell, rent, or transmit personal data.

Data Collection

Zero Reader does not collect personal information, reading content, reading history, device identifiers, diagnostics, analytics, or usage data.

Local Storage

Books, reading progress, bookmarks, annotations, reading statistics, reader preferences, custom fonts, and reader backgrounds are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded by Zero Reader.

Network Access

Zero Reader does not make network requests for app functionality and does not send your data to servers controlled by the developer or by third parties.

Tracking and Third Parties

  • No advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics SDKs.
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • No third-party data sharing.

Children's Privacy

Zero Reader does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact me@yinmo19.top.
